## Troubleshooting

- I don't have any information on that

> Commands are created automatically after the device is refreshed for the first time.

- I don't have automatic updates

> Make sure the "Auto-refresh" value is not empty
>
> <img src="../{{site.img}}/horoscope_actualisation.png" alt="Auto-refresh" width="900" />

- No information is displayed on the dashboard

> <img src="../{{site.img}}/horoscope_dashboard.png" alt="Dashboard" width="350" />


> Check the boxes next to the desired information for the device
>
> ![Command](../{{site.img}}/horoscope_command.png)
